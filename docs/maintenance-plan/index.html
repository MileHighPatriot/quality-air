What happens at a tune-up

Furnace, in the fall

  • Burner and flame sensor cleaned
  • Heat exchanger checked for cracks
  • Carbon monoxide (CO) test

AC, in the spring

  • Coils cleaned
  • Refrigerant and electrical check
  • Drain line cleared

Why a plan pays for itself

  1. 1

    It catches small problems early.

    A worn part found at a tune-up is a small repair. The same part failing in January is a no-heat call.

  2. 2

    It keeps the warranty valid.

    Most manufacturers ask for proof of yearly maintenance. We keep the record for you.

  3. 3

    You're first in line on the coldest night.

    When every furnace in town quits at once, plan members get scheduled first.

Questions people ask

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. We just ask for 30 days' notice.

What if I have two systems?

Each furnace and AC pair counts as one system. A second system is added to the same plan at a reduced rate, and we tune up both on the same visit.

Does it cover parts?

No. The plan gives you a discount on repairs (10% or 15%), not free parts. You'll see the price range before any work starts.

Do you work on any brand?

Yes. Our techs service all major furnace, AC, and heat pump brands.
